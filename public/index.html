Hi, I'm Orchid.

I am a principal research engineer at Amazon. I work on training and optimizing large-scale multi-modal foundational models as part of the Amazon AGI organization.

In the past, I was part of AWS AI Labs where I was working on on few-shot learning, meta-learning, AutoML and seq2seq models. I obtained my MS from Columbia University and BS from Jadavpur University, both in CS.
